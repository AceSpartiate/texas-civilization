    For teachers and IT: the game runs on the teacher's laptop, on the classroom network (port 1835 unless the Host page says otherwise). Student devices must be able to reach that laptop: client or AP isolation off between them on this network, or a rule allowing that TCP port to the laptop's address; and Windows Firewall on the laptop must allow Node.js on the Private profile.
