Final image prompt: "Wide 16:9 background painting for a Texas Revolution 1835/36 family-creation dice screen, professional hand-painted 2D storybook game art in the same warm outlined frontier style as an illustrated top-down historical game. Scene: early autumn sunrise over the Guadalupe River country, a modest 1830s settler wagon and two oxen on a rutted road in lower left, a small family standing together beside it at a distance, live oak and pecan trees framing the upper corners, a ford crossing the shallow river on the right, low timbered ridges, meadow grasses and a few simple split-rail fences. Historically plausible frontier clothing and transport, no iconic Alamo facade, no flags. Composition must have a quiet, low-detail CENTRAL AREA for a wide overlay card and enough darker scenery at the outer edges to frame it. Soft golden light, sage green, weathered indigo, ochre, warm parchment, dark umber, subtle paper texture and crisp painterly ink outlines. Rich environmental detail but calm, legible silhouettes, cohesive premium indie-game illustration. Absolutely no text, lettering, logos, dice, interface panels, modern structures, modern vehicles or watermark."

`#creation[data-roll-visible=true]` holds this background before **and after** the dice settle. `renderFamilyRoll` owns that state, because the underlying creation step may already advance to `surname` while the rolled result waits for *Meet your family*. The curtain title is reduced to a masthead. `#family-roll` is a wider parchment card: one die card for the family, one for starting means when the server provides that die, then the server's result and one action button. The d20s are CSS, with a calmer alternating tumble; reduced motion remains governed by `public/app.js`. Single-die legacy classes still hide the second card. No die outcome or server wording changed.

Validation: `npm run test:creation` (10 checks); `npm run test:creation-screen` (24 step/size measurements at 1366×768, 1024×768, and 390×844); `npm run test:means` (roll and transport outcomes); `node --test tests/creation-words.test.mjs` (7 checks). Screenshots: `docs/evidence/creation-die.png` and `creation-die-means.png`. The desktop game is the supported target; the narrow size is a regression check.
