# Second-cast dialogue and listening — 21 September 2026

`people-cast2-dialogue.png` adds sixteen transparent conversation poses for `rust-woman`, `indigo`, `ochre` and `blue-girl`. Each identity receives a two-frame east-facing speaking cycle, a south/front listening pose and a north/back listening pose. The renderer may mirror the speaking frames for west-facing conversations.

The accepted atlas preserves the established cast identities and clothing while giving speech a readable gesture change from hand-to-chest to open palm. Listening poses stay restrained so the renderer's breathing motion can add life without changing the meaning of the pose. These clips visualize conversations only; the simulation remains authoritative for who heard a messenger, when knowledge arrived, and what information a character may repeat.

The built-in image generator produced the art from the accepted `people-cast2-idle` identity reference. A second built-in spacing pass reduced each sprite within its cell. The final **1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG** was copied unchanged. Read-only inspection measured **76.2470% fully transparent pixels** and all sixteen required figures. The central art registry, generated manifests and browser proof are intentionally left to the integration pass.
