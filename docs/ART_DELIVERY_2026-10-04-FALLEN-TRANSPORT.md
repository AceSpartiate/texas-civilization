# Covered fallen transport

Eight composite frames provide four-pose volunteer and Mexican regular bearer cycles: `volunteer-fallen-carry` and `regular-fallen-carry`. Each contains two bearers and a fully covered, still body. These are distinct from living-wounded art. East faces right; mirror for west. Garments and gestures are interpretive game art.

Named and family-specific transport art remains separate work; do not present this anonymous bundle as a portrait.
