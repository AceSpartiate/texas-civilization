# Texas Revolution — 15 September 2026

Release tag: `v2026.09.15.1`.

This update brings the unpublished settling, colonies and woods work together with the latest artwork. New families arrive by wagon, choose their house site, survey and clear their land, hunt their own woods, fell trees and haul logs home. The new house plot supports period plans and individual pieces, with construction progressing through stages and using the family's logs. Existing saved classes keep their previous house system.

News travels between settlements and reaches families through riders. The update also fixes a rider passing somebody on the last stretch before a relay, and invalid river crossings on the invented Gonzales map.

Gonzales has a fuller settlement, Béxar has its town assembly, and the Alamo workshop includes roof cutaways and independent north-wall collapse previews. River banks, roads and fords share the improved landscape rendering. Timber bridge rendering is available for explicitly defined bridge sites; no bridge was invented on the map. The art library includes the latest livestock, land-clearing and character-care sheets: 45 atlases, 663 sprites and 283 animation clips.

## Install or update

Download `TexasRevolutionSetup.exe` from this release, or use **Check for updates** in an installed launcher. Stop the class before updating. The regular ZIP includes the runtime and is the updater's download; the `NeedsNode` ZIP is for a machine with Node 22 or later already installed. Saved classes are excluded from all three packages.

## Scope

This is a development prototype. Later Revolution campaigns are not playable yet. The Alamo workshop's room and wall interactions are previews rather than classroom battle mechanics. Water highlights remain static, and several building and character actions still use documented stand-ins. School-network and physical-device acceptance remain outstanding. The Windows installer is unsigned.

Release verification is recorded in `HANDOFF.md` and the release evidence file after the build completes.
