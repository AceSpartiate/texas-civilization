# News carried by people

Owner direction, 2026-09-10. This is a required product direction for Claude, with production acceptance criteria. It extends [VISION.md](../VISION.md)'s information → pressure → choice → physical action loop. It is **not a claim that the conversation system or later campaigns already exist**. Read this before extending news, couriers, opportunities, government participation or the Runaway Scrape.

## The experience to build

An event happens somewhere in the shared world. A person witnesses it or receives a report, mounts a horse and follows a real route. Near Gonzales, a family member meets the rider early. Farther away, a family near Liberty hears an older account much later, perhaps from a different carrier who has already repeated it to other people. The player learns through a conversation between that particular family member and messenger. The existing summary such as “Mexican detachment withdraws” belongs in the family's remembered record after receipt; it must cease being the main dramatic delivery.

The rider approaches visibly, slows and faces the person who can hear them. A restrained arrival cue and an accessible “Elena has met a rider” invitation guide the player to the encounter. Opening it frames the two people and reveals the speaker's words, where the report came from and how old it is. It must not reveal the news in the invitation. The player can ask useful questions, listen, defer, or leave. Dialogue exposes uncertainty through natural answers: what the rider saw, what someone else claimed, when they left, and what they do not know. It never offers omniscient answers just because the server has the truth.

An illustrative, **fictional** exchange might ask “Did you see them leave?” and receive “I saw them move away from the crossing. I cannot tell you where they are now.” Final lines require contextual writing and historical review. Do not make every messenger deliver the same four questions or attach a quiz, correctness reward or patriotic score. Conversation must change what the household understands and what it can consider doing.

The intended emotional arc is attachment, interrupted work, concern, competing obligations, uncertainty and sometimes fear or frustration. During Houston's retreat, families may be exasperated that they cannot learn his plans while people and property are at risk. That experience should arise from dated, incomplete reports and the player's physical circumstances. Do not assert that Houston literally communicated with nobody, invent a secret strategic certainty, or manufacture panic with false countdowns and forced casualties. A calm player who chooses to shelter others or remain with family must still have a meaningful story.

## Geography creates different family stories

| Where and when a family becomes involved | Opportunities and inherited consequences |
| --- | --- |
| Near early events around Gonzales and Béxar | Earlier witness encounters, supply appeals and practical chances to travel or serve. A family member may subsequently be present at the Alamo or with forces caught in the Goliad campaign. Absence, capture, death and survivors' reports must follow that person's actual commitments, location and documented circumstances. No quota requires a nearby household to lose someone. |
| Farther east, including Liberty | Later reports of western events, refugees carrying new accounts, and different remaining travel windows. Service with Houston's army, care, transport, food and protection of evacuating NPC and player households become plausible opportunities. Distance changes the opportunity set; it is not a punishment for receiving news late. |
| Along evacuation routes and crossings | Escort, reunite separated people, share scarce wagon capacity, find shelter and aid the injured. Existing people, animals, promises, goods and relationships continue through the Runaway Scrape. Protecting someone has a concrete recipient and consequence, not an abstract escort score. |
| Near communities represented at Washington-on-the-Brazos | A geographically and demographically plausible opportunity to accompany, supply, carry messages for, or serve in government work. An actual delegate role requires a researched seat and eligibility model; proximity alone does not create extra delegates or displace named historical people. Government participants encounter sourced debates and pressures concerning the army and the need to fight. |
| Later approach to San Jacinto | Those who can physically arrive may serve, support or fight; other family members can continue evacuation or civilian work. The same earlier choices determine who is available. The victory does not restore dead people, abandoned property or broken commitments. |

Use allocation at class creation to provide contrasting starts across 5–30 households, normally 11–15. Validate population, routes, time windows and role capacity rather than handing every region an identical quest. Equal worth does not mean identical timing or access to every event. Allow stories to cross through real meetings, aid and messages. No late join button may teleport a person into the army, convention or battlefield.

The convention's delegates were elected, and 59 ultimately attended; these are historical constraints, not available player slots. See [TSHA: Convention of 1836](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/convention-of-1836). Government work continued while its location changed during the retreat; distinguish the convention from the later administration. See [TSHA: Ad Interim Government](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/ad-interim-government). The [THC Runaway Scrape marker](https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/Details/5291009683) connects reports of the Alamo, the army's retreat and flight eastward, including people east of Liberty. These checks support the direction; exact dialogue, travel speeds, dated letters, delegate eligibility and individual outcomes still require registered claims in [HISTORY.md](../HISTORY.md).

## The implementation contract

Keep one authoritative event, one persistent carrier and one route. A dispatch records its origin, departure time, permitted report/version and intended route or audience. A rider must start at the actual source or a modeled relay point; do not spawn an invisible independent rider at each recipient's boundary. Relays preserve source ancestry and observation age. Routes, travel speed, impediments and arrival govern delay, using world miles and simulation minutes rather than a region-specific UI timer.

Add a server-owned encounter record referencing carrier ID, hearing family-member ID, report version, location, time and conversation progress. Detect range against their actual positions and terrain/visibility rules, including crossings and room boundaries. Nearness through an impassable wall or across an uncrossed river is insufficient. A household's camera position never counts as a listener. One rider can inform successive eligible listeners; deduplicate a repeated report for the same audience without deleting a genuinely new account or correction.

Separate **encounter available**, **words spoken/heard**, **household knowledge**, and **player presentation read**. Specify the receipt point: an opening spoken line may establish the core heard fact, while later questions add qualified details. Merely approaching within range cannot inject the whole report. Once a person has heard something, failing to click the UI must not erase their knowledge. Persist queued presentation for reconnect and deferred reading. Decide explicitly which essential spoken content can proceed while the player attends another family member; do not pause the entire class for one conversation or make every historical outcome wait for reading.

The server projects only permitted speakers, encounter IDs, eligible lines, choices and heard facts. Do not ship future branches, secret report payloads, distant destinations or corrected truth inside hidden HTML/JSON. A viewer who sees a rider may know only that a rider is there. Local family knowledge and household sharing need an explicit rule when family members are separated; no unexplained telepathic relay. Preserve existing household-level reports as a migration boundary until person-level hearing/sharing is deliberately implemented and tested.

Persist source → dispatch → journey → encounter → heard statement → pressure → choice → action → consequence. Reconnect resumes the same encounter without replaying its effects; commands are idempotent. Time compression stops at pending meaningful contact, departure, danger and opportunity deadlines. Preserve missed, delayed, corrected and unanswered messages through time jumps. A lost or diverted carrier has a visible causal history, not a silent dice roll. Final revelation compares event time, report provenance, actual receipt and the family's decisions without revealing hidden facts during play.

An autonomous director schedules historically grounded pressures and usable encounters. It cannot inject teacher-triggered messages or force predetermined family tragedies. Arrival of news may close an opportunity that has already passed, but must communicate that loss honestly and offer locally plausible remaining work. High-stakes choices show understood risks; hidden punitive randomness cannot manufacture the desired emotional arc.

## Production quality: release gates, not a visual label

“AAA quality” here means the following work is complete, coherent and tested. It is not a promise that the current prototype meets these gates.

| Gate | Required proof before calling the feature complete |
| --- | --- |
| Causal delivery | Replay the same seeded dispatch to a nearby and distant listener. Arrival follows measured route/time; changing road distance or blocking a crossing changes receipt. Assert no payload leakage on every pre-contact snapshot, including Host. |
| Real encounters | Walk a family member into and out of range; test two listeners, a blocked wall, a missed rider, interruption and a relay. IDs and speaker relationships persist; no duplicated carrier or report effects. |
| Conversation craft | Author and review opening, questions, uncertainty, refusal/defer, repeat/correction and interrupted resumption for each important encounter. Speakers remember prior exchanges. No placeholder barks, obvious repetition, unearned certainty or contemporary exposition dumps in final content. |
| Performance and staging | Rider/horse contact, stops, facing, speaking/listening and departures align without sliding, popping or mounted/dismounted duplication. At 30 clients on the target managed Chromebook, meet a measured 30 fps presentation target with readable figures and no sustained input delay; use stronger desktop motion only within a tested budget. |
| Attention and access | No camera hijack while dragging or editing an order. Queue simultaneous arrivals; prioritize without showing secrets. Test keyboard-only, touch, screen reader, reduced motion, pause and optional sound off. Text has no forced reading timer; optional voice has synchronized captions and cannot carry unique information. |
| Persistence | Save/reload mid-ride, mid-conversation, after hearing a line and during an interrupted question. Check refresh, dropped SSE and time jumps. No repeated reward, erased hearing, duplicated participant or retroactive knowledge. |
| Regional pacing | Simulate 5, 11–15 and 30 households with near/far starts and mixed/refuse/idle strategies over the intended 45-minute lesson. Review actual arrival distributions, reachable opportunities, quiet stretches and concurrent demands. Distant families remain consequential without receiving instant western news. |
| Historical and emotional review | Register factual claims, mark fictional dialogue and resolve disputed details. Playtest whether students can explain why their family knew less, what they chose and what followed. Fear and frustration emerge from stakes and uncertainty; refusing service remains valid. |
