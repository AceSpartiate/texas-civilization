The public funeral is the core documented sequence. Seguín's contemporary account says ashes were found in **three places**, ashes from the two smaller heaps were placed in a black-covered coffin, the coffin was brought to the parish church, and a public procession gave military honors. The cutscene's solitary gathering gesture is **dramatized**; there is no evidence he secretly rescued ashes from the ordered ceremony.
