# Biome ground cover and Béxar details — 2026-09-21

`biome-ground-bexar.png` adds sixteen transparent environment sprites for natural-region identity and the irrigated landscape around San Antonio de Béxar.

The sheet contains palmetto, cypress knees, two cane clumps and a wind pose, tall prairie grass and a wind pose, two thorn-thicket variants, Spanish dagger, marsh cordgrass, dune grass, three compatible acequia pieces, and a period brush fence. The acequia pieces share the same water width and bank treatment so the layout can join them without changing art style.

The accepted built-in ImageGen output was copied unchanged. Gameplay remains responsible for biome choice, local weather, field geometry, and whether a crossing is passable.
