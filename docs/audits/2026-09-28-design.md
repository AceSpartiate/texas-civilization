# Design audit: can the game be played as intended? — 2026-09-28

**Asked by the owner (2026-09-28):** "check for problems with the game. we're specifically looking for things that would
prevent the game from being played as intended. gaps in game design logic, things we may have forgot or not thought about."

**This audit's slice:** game-design logic and the student's experience, measured against `VISION.md`, period by period.
Read-only: nothing in `sim/`, `public/`, `server/` or the other docs was changed. Tree audited: main `aa35088`
(v2026.09.28.1). Line numbers are at that commit.

**Method.** VISION.md in full, CLAUDE.md's read-list docs where each finding needed them, HANDOFF.md's top sections, then
the code. Every finding is traced to code lines. Findings marked *(re-read)* were checked line by line a second time for this
report; a few were confirmed with a read-only `node -e` of the modules (said where). Measured numbers are quoted from the
repository's own evidence (`docs/evidence/battle-class-time.json`, `docs/BALANCE.md`) and were not re-run. Same computer
only; nothing here is a classroom observation, and no LAN or district claim is made.

**Severity.**
- *Blocker*: a VISION Tier 1 principle cannot be met as built, or a class cannot be run the way the design assumes.
- *Serious*: the principle is met badly, a student is misled, or a real class will be confused.
- *Minor*: wrong words, stale documents, small traps.

**Owner decisions.** Several findings touch decisions already on record: coin for cotton outside the keeper's purse
(2026-09-16), staying home not winning a class of fifteen (2026-09-27), the tutorial never reopened by the teacher
(2026-09-27), women not offered the fighting (2026-09-16), the burn zone dealt by join order (2026-09-26). They are listed
for what they **combine into** with other rules, not to reopen them. Each one says so.

## 1. The whole class: pacing, periods, the teacher

### 1.1 B1 — Period 1 is about two class meetings; the whole game about five

**Where.** `server/app.mjs:161` (Study = 9.5 s a tick); `sim/clock.mjs:34-44` (calendar scales, still citing the
"fifty-minute budget" and "to come with build step 5/6"); `docs/evidence/battle-class-time.json` (measured); owner's budget
`docs/COLONIES.md` §5.7 and §7e ("Each day stays near fifty minutes").

| Period / phase | Dates | 1 tick = | Unheld, Study | Measured, Study (nobody played / a played man in every fight) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 home | dawn Sep 28 → Sep 29, 10 a.m. | 20 min | 13.3 min | |
| P1 news | Sep 29 → Oct 3 | 60 min | 18.6 + Gonzales ~5 | |
| P1 gathering | Oct 3 → Oct 13 | 4 h | 9.5 | |
| P1 campaign | Oct 13 → Dec 15 evening | 12 h | 20.2 + Concepción, Grass Fight, Béxar ~22 | |
| **P1** | | | 61.6 | **88.7 / 103** (560 ticks measured) |
| winter gap | Dec 15 → Jan 25 | skipped (`sim/periods.mjs:63`) | 0 | |
| **P2** | Jan 25 → night of Mar 13 | 20 min, then 12 h | 31.8 + Alamo and the south ~16 | **47 / 56** |
| **P3** | Mar 14 → Apr 25 | 4 h throughout | 39.9 + Coleto, Goliad, San Jacinto ~22 | **62 / 69** |
| **Game** | | | | **198 / 229** |

Brisk is about ×0.42 (P1 37-43 min); Quick ×0.1. These are lower bounds: holds from students at their screens (1.4) are not in
them.

**Why it prevents play as intended.** VISION §1 (≈45 minutes) and the owner's three-period model (COLONIES §7e) assume each
period fits one class. At Study a 45-minute class reaches about mid-October, and **nothing tells the teacher that Brisk is
the pace that fits**. With B2, the natural thing a teacher does at the bell destroys the class.

**Direction.** Re-budget each period against 50 minutes with played-family holds measured, make Brisk the default or say on
the Host page what fits, or add a planned mid-period stop.

### 1.2 B2 — No "stop for today"; End Game is one click and final *(re-read)*

**Where.** `public/index.html:122` (`<button data-action="end">End Game</button>`); `public/app.js:132` (`confirmLabel` has
`new-class`, `stop-server`, `send-for`, `winter-recall`, `flee`, `flight-stay`, `road-abandon` - no `end`); `server/app.mjs:1046`
sets `status='ended'`; `sim/periods.mjs:29-35` `canContinue` needs the period's end milestone, so an ended mid-period class
can never continue; `public/ending.js:82` then says "How the families finished" (final, not interim).

**Why.** The obvious button at the bell ends the war for good, names a final winner from October glory alone, and skips the
Scrape and the ending the game is built for. The only safe route (Pause → Stop Server → relaunch → Resume) is documented
nowhere as "continue tomorrow". VISION §4 *Autonomous Host* and §18 (Pause, Resume, End Game, Settings) assume End Game means
the end of the whole story.

**Direction.** An "End for today" that pauses and saves; a double-press confirm on End Game saying it is final; allow a class
ended mid-period to be continued.

### 1.3 S1 — An unattended class runs on, and people die unwatched

**Where.** `server/app.mjs:191` ("a class never does" auto-pause - only solo); `markAbsences` `server/app.mjs:288-297` hands
every family to the director after two minutes; `sim/clock.mjs:111` - absent families do not hold the clock.

**Why.** A teacher who forgets Pause at the bell (likely, given B1/B2) returns to a period that has ended and to men who died
at Béxar with no student watching - exactly VISION §17's "Several weeks pass. Thomas died."

**Direction.** Auto-pause a class when no played family has been present for N minutes.

### 1.4 S2 — One student can hold the whole class for minutes

**Where.** Only military questions and calls have a real-time budget (`sim/decision-budget.mjs:32`, `:42`). Not budgeted:
an open rider conversation holds the clock (`sim/clock.mjs:111`) for up to 60 ticks of patience (`sim/encounters.mjs:88`),
**9.5 real minutes at Study**, reset by each question asked (the comment at `encounters.mjs:83`, "a minute of a real
classroom", dates from the one-second tick); the spring **order to leave** holds the class at 20-minute ticks for
`FLIGHT_PATIENCE` (1440 min, `sim/auto.mjs:71`) = 72 ticks, **11.4 real minutes**, on each of six order days
(`sim/scrape.mjs:59-75`); a played man marching or walking home drops the whole class to 2-hour ticks (`sim/military-pacing.mjs:101`,
a known `ceiling:`).

**Why.** Thirty students wait on one; the budgets in B1 grow further.

**Direction.** Put conversations and the flight order on the same real-time budget as the military questions.

### 1.5 S3 — The teacher has no guide to the game as it now is

**Where.** The teacher packages carry only `README.md`, `GAME.md` and `HISTORY.md` (README.md:61). README.md:7 "a prototype
of one afternoon", README.md:45 "the slice lasts about 54 minutes ... It stops after the fight". GAME.md:17 "An end-of-class
outcome that names a winning family is planned and not built"; GAME.md:200 "Only the household's principal can be given
orders today"; GAME.md:239 the 4 min 44 s slice; GAME.md:285 "Two class periods" (there are three); GAME.md:319 says the 45-minute
pacing, the Runaway Scrape payoff and the epilogues are "not implemented". `sim/winter.mjs:21-23` and `sim/directors.mjs:144`
say period 2 ends Feb 23 (it ends Mar 13); `docs/COLONIES.md` §6m says the second period ends with "the **final**
reckoning".

**Why.** "Things we plainly forgot: the teacher's debrief." A teacher cannot learn from the shipped documents that the game
is three meetings (really more, B1), that Continue then Resume starts each, which pace fits, what to do at the bell, or how
to run the discussion at the end (§5.4).

**Direction.** A one-page teacher guide in the package: the periods, Continue/Resume, Pause at the bell, Brisk, the debrief.
Retire the stale lines in README and GAME.md.

### 1.6 Minor

- **M1** The pace is not saved with the class (`server/app.mjs:1039-1042`): a teacher who chose Brisk reopens tomorrow at Study.
- **M2** `sim/clock.mjs:38-44`'s table still says "to come with build step 5/6"; period 3 is one 4-hour scale throughout (`sim/periods.mjs:144`).
- **M3** `public/ending.js:87` "press Continue when the class meets again" implies one meeting per period (B1).

## 2. The first hour: the guided start, and what it leaves untaught

The tutorial (`sim/lesson.mjs:176-296`, `docs/LESSON.md` §2) is ten farm steps: arrive and site the house, give an order,
build, survey, clear, plant (crop choice), harvest, sell, hunt, well. Its closing card says "That is the whole of it. The farm
is yours to run now" (`sim/lesson.mjs:364`).

### 2.1 B8 — Nothing after the ten farm steps teaches the rest of the game

**Where.** `sim/lesson.mjs:364`; the only just-in-time help is in the lesson strip (`public/app.js:5678-5719`) and goes with it.

| Later system | How a student meets it | Taught? |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement call / turn out | a "!" on each eligible row; menu on press (`public/family-panel.js:444`, `app.js:4529`); no Messages card | no |
| Army questions, the 90 s lapse | Messages card; lapse warned only after 2/3 of the time (`public/military-attention.js:36-38`) | no |
| Main-person star | ☆/★, meaning in hover text only (`app.js:4197-4198`) | no |
| Flight order, load, stay | "!" on the main person's row; the card only when that person is selected (`app.js:3852`) | no |
| Route east, "Change where we go", tapping the map | main person's card (`app.js:3897-3899`, `3956`); map tap only with the editor open (`app.js:1695`) | no |
| Stop and rest a day / Camp apart from the crowd | road-only icons; cost in hover popup (`sim/disease.mjs:784-796`) | no |
| Doctor's bark, calomel, rice and tea | lines in the town errand; what each does only in `title=` (`public/errand.js:111`) | no |
| Minding babies | "with the baby to mind" on the row (`sim/chores.mjs:2132`); the fix only in a hover popup | no |
| Obedience | lines on the row and journal only, by design | no |
| Coin vs food at the counter | toggles (`errand.js:115`); tutorial says "Payment in food or coin both count" (`lesson.mjs:273`) | misleading (3.6) |
| Trading with students / helping raise walls | only when standing with another family's person (`app.js:3687-3692`) | no |
| ¡Alto! halt or run | main person's card; no Messages card | no |
| Family key | bottom of the journal (`index.html:554`) | no |

**Why.** VISION §9: "students should struggle with decisions, not controls". Every system that decides the war, the flight and
the ending is met cold, most of them at the moments of greatest pressure (§7).

**Direction.** A non-gating "what's new" card the first time each later system appears (first call, first army question,
the flight order, the first road question, the first sickness), pointing at the control.

### 2.2 S4 — The X in minute one, and no help anywhere

**Where.** `docs/LESSON.md` §1 and §6; `public/app.js:5850-5862` (the older walk-through is marked seen and never offered);
the X is the same "×" as every popup's close (`public/index.html:408`); after 5 minutes nothing remains.

**Why.** Children close popups by habit. The owner has ruled out a teacher reopening the tutorial; a reference that does not
gate anything is not ruled out, and none exists.

**Direction.** Label it "Stop the guide", and keep a small permanent "What can I do?" reference (owner's call).

### 2.3 S5 — The first big decision has only a small "!"

**Where.** `public/military-attention.js` raises a Messages card for a rider and for army questions but none for a
settlement call; `public/family-panel.js:444`. The call lapses 5 real minutes after it reaches the family
(`sim/decision-budget.mjs`), paused only while the student is in the tutorial - whose strip never mentions it.

**Why.** Turning out is the choice most of the ending hangs on (glory), and a student can miss it entirely.

**Direction.** A Messages card for the call; a line in the tutorial strip when one arrives.

### 2.4 S6 — Decisive information is hover-only

**Where.** The errand's `does` (what the doctor's calomel does, which `sim/shops.mjs:209-210` says is "said before the choice")
is a `title=` only (`public/errand.js:111`). Tapping an open icon sends the order at once; its cost is in the hover popup
(`public/app.js:4549`, `4842`) - e.g. "No miles are made ... the Mexican army does not halt" on "Stop and rest a day". The
star, the "!" and auto explain themselves only in `title=` (`app.js:4192-4210`).

**Why.** Chromebook touchpads need a hover-wait and touch screens have none; the "cost before you choose" rule (VISION §4
legible causality) is kept only for mouse users.

**Direction.** Show `does` as visible text; first tap opens the popup with a Send button.

### 2.5 S7 — Trading and neighbours' house-raisings are nearly impossible to find

**Where.** `public/app.js:3687-3692`: trade needs one of yours and another family's person at the same place, then a click on
theirs; families are spread across the colonies. Nothing says a neighbour is building.

**Why.** VISION §22's "another player's wagon" and §8's "community relationships" need students to meet; the classroom
network VISION §10 counts on has no in-game handle.

**Direction.** "Trade" on the journal's *Also here* list; a journal line when a neighbour starts raising walls.

### 2.6 Minor

- **M4** Reading level of key prompts: `sim/calls.mjs:35` ("An express has brought word ... The committee asks the men to turn
  out"), `sim/calls.mjs:45` ("as speedily as they can"), `sim/army.mjs:762` ("paraded ... under a commander they elect"),
  `sim/army.mjs:1071` ("winter quarters ... in squads"). Keep the period words; add a one-line plain gloss.
- **M5** The family key is never shown at join (`public/index.html:554`); show it once at the end of creation.
- **M6** `docs/LESSON.md` §6 still says the sell step "is finished by coin"; the as-built step accepts food too.
- **M7** Obedience and baby-minding cause effects the student cannot trace to a fix; a child switching its own automation
  off raises no "!" (`sim/obedience.mjs`, `sim/childhood.mjs:77-83`).

## 3. The 1835 campaign and the winter

### 3.1 B9 — The farm is a coin pump with no season *(re-read)*

**Where.** `sim/chores.mjs:66` `RIPEN_TICKS = 18`; `:2496` ripens by tick count, reading no date or season. 18 ticks is about
3 real minutes at Study: 6 hours of 1835 in the farming day, 9 days in the campaign, 3 days in the spring. `sim/lesson.mjs:260`
tells the student the crop is "some weeks ripening". The store buys cotton and food for coin **outside** its keeper's purse
(`sim/errands.mjs:66` `OUTSIDE_PURSE`, owner 2026-09-16), `STORE_BALE_COIN = 2` (`sim/shops.mjs:102`).

**Why.** Together (the purse rule is the owner's; the tick ripening is not documented as a choice) they make a field on auto
an unbounded coin source: `docs/BALANCE.md` §9.3 has class winners at 122k-347k, against a starting 3-10 reales, so the
second die the owner added (FAMILY_CREATION, 2026-09-25) does almost nothing. Corn in January, harvests every few minutes,
and "the harvest may not come in before the day ends" (GAME.md:168) is not true. VISION §4 legible causality and §19 ("people
made decisions without knowing how it would end") need a harvest that can fail to come.

**Direction.** Ripen by calendar days with one harvest window a season, refuse planting out of season, and/or cap what the
store pays in coin per period.

**Answered 2026-09-28 (owner: "Seasons and a limited market") and built on a worktree branch, not released**: `sim/seasons.mjs`,
`sim/market.mjs`; measured in docs/BALANCE.md §10.

### 3.2 S8 — The tutorial's gate refuses the war and the sickroom *(re-read)*

**Where.** `sim/lesson.mjs:68-88` (`ALWAYS`) has the calls, army and camp answers, the courier, the road and the flight, but not
the winter's chores `chore:enlist-regular`, `enlist-auxiliary`, `join-garrison`, `join-matamoros`, `go-vote`, `join-relief`,
`join-houston` (`sim/winter.mjs:55`), nor `chore:nurse-home`. `lessonRefusal` (`sim/lesson.mjs:389-400`) refuses them "Not yet -
first, bring the crop in." (confirmed by a read-only `node -e` at the harvest step). `docs/LESSON.md` §6: "A family still
mid-lesson in January is still mid-lesson"; the lesson never ends at a period boundary (`advanceLesson`, `lesson.mjs:409`).
Sickness can kill from period 2 (`sim/disease.mjs:204`).

**Why.** It breaks the file's own rule ("Nothing the game itself asks a family is ever refused", `lesson.mjs:25`) and can let
a slow reader's child die because "Nurse the sick" is greyed. The period-1 median family's house stands about tick 99
(HANDOFF.md:3454) against 84 quiet ticks, so a typical tutorial student is on step 3 when the news comes.

**Direction.** Add the winter and Houston joins and `nurse-home` to `ALWAYS`, and end the gate when period 2 opens.

### 3.3 S9 — A family that turned nobody out has nothing to decide for most of the campaign

**Where.** Calls lapse after 5 real minutes (`sim/decision-budget.mjs:42`); army questions go only to members
(`sim/military-pacing.mjs:38-47`); `followTheArmy` moves only those who promised (`sim/army.mjs:160-167`).

**Why.** From Oct 13 to Dec 15 - 45-60 real minutes at Study, including about 22 minutes of battle holds it is not shown
(`sim/directors.mjs:1523`) - such a family has farming, hunting and trade, and no decision the war puts to it. VISION §8 and §12
("every household should receive several meaningful opportunities").

**Direction.** A late path to the army (the October reinforcements), or local events for the families at home (supplying
the army, a sick neighbour, a rumour to check).

### 3.4 S10 — Glory rewards the "right" answer *(re-read)*

**Where.** `sim/camp.mjs:253-255`: at the fork on April 16 a man who answers **yes** to the right-hand road to Harrisburg is
awarded glory (`role: 'forward'`, weight 2, `sim/glory.mjs:40`). `sim/army.mjs:939`: saying you would go in when Béxar's storming
was ordered earns `willing` (2).

**Why.** VISION §11 allows glory as "participation of every kind, not obedience to a request"; §22 "Nobody asks: choose the
correct historical answer." Both awards pay for one answer to a question the army asks. Once glory is visible (B4), students
learn it.

**Direction.** Award the event (being at the fork, at the parade), not the answer; or drop the two roles.

### 3.5 S11 — Food never constrains a family at home

**Where.** `sim/routines.mjs:57-60`: `fed = food + (workers - eaten) × days`; every grown person "working about the place" (the
default task, `sim/world.mjs:189`) adds 1 food a day and eats 0.35 (`sim/family.mjs:555`). A read-only `node -e` check: a
default family at home gains 6 food over 10 days with nobody farming, loses 14 when everyone rests.

**Why.** The corn-or-cotton choice is a non-decision (cotton is worth about ten times corn); seed paid in food makes the cotton
loop cost nothing; hunger bites only on the road east. VISION §8 lists food first among noncombat decisions.

**Direction.** Make working about the place a small fraction of a ration, so the field, the hunt and the herd feed the family.

### 3.6 S12 — Coin: no required use, every spend hurts, and nobody is told it is the score

**Where.** `sim/ending.mjs:45` (`coin × kept × (1 + glory) + land`); the hoe mended free at home (`sim/shops.mjs:181`), seed
payable in food, a lost rifle hurting only hunting (`sim/keeping.mjs:173-187`); nothing before the ending (`public/`,
`sim/lesson.mjs`) says coin is what counts, and bales in the house count nothing; the tutorial says food and coin "both count"
(`sim/lesson.mjs:273`).

**Why.** VISION §20 amendment: "Money is visible throughout and must be used — traded or spent." As built the rational play is
never to spend, and every trade of coin to another family helps a rival. Students choose at the counter blind to the stakes.

**Direction.** One plain line at the counter ("Coin is what the family will have to show"), and at least one expense a family
must meet (winter supplies, a tax, powder), or count goods at store prices.

### 3.7 S13 — A wounded man at Béxar is shut in the Alamo without warning

**Where.** `sim/army.mjs:1059-1063` (a dangerous wound: 60 days lying at Béxar); `sim/periods.mjs:95-107` (a man still mending
stays where he is over the winter, *re-read*); `sim/routines.mjs:70` (he turns well with no line and nothing starts him home);
`sim/alamo.mjs:88-103` (`beginSiege` puts everybody standing at Béxar into the garrison); `sim/alamo.mjs:302-309` (every
fighting-age man inside falls). Blas Herrera's warning goes only to men formally in the garrison (`sim/surprise.mjs:72-76`);
the winter opens with "The men who took Béxar are home" (`sim/periods.mjs:111`).

**Why.** VISION §4: "Do not use hidden punitive RNG to create arbitrary tragedy." The family never chose the Alamo.

**Direction.** When the wound mends, say so and start him home, as the army's break-up does.

### 4.1 B5 — Nothing a family did for another family is ever read again *(re-read)*

**Where.** `household.relationships.neighbor += reachedCamp ? 2 : 1` (`sim/directors.mjs:746`, commented "the relationship that
later arcs inherit") is the only write, and nothing in `sim/`, `public/` or `server/` reads it. Wall-raising help
(`sim/houses.mjs:206-243`) and help in town (`sim/town-scenes.mjs:1046-1069`) leave a story line only; trade offers lapse the

(`sim/calls.mjs:230-232`) and never shown. On the road the only exchange is `share-food` (`sim/flight-work.mjs:408-419`): one
food to the hungriest family camped there, not chosen by the student, nothing returned. No family can carry another's people
or goods, travel with another, or lend a person or an ox.

**Why.** VISION §4 (persistent consequences: relationships), §21 ("relationships affect later assistance"), §22 ("Another player's

**Direction.** A ledger of help between households that the Scrape reads: a seat or a load in a named family's wagon,

### 4.2 B6 — The homecoming almost never happens, and nothing comes after it *(re-read)*

**Where.** `turnHome` (`sim/scrape.mjs:510-536`) runs once, at the victory word (`sim/directors.mjs:202` `victory-word`

has no home site to choose. No period follows (`sim/periods.mjs:29-35`).

**Why.** VISION §22 ("A student looks at the same farm their family has lived on since the beginning"), §17 (no convenient
teleport home, but the journey home is the story's close). The unburned house, the cache dug up (`digUpCache`), the herd
found again (`findStockAgain`), the milk cow home - all built, rarely reached.

**Direction.** End the class on the homecoming rather than a date; turn every family home; a short aftermath at the farm.

### 4.3 B7 — "Leave for the east" abandons everything by default *(re-read)*

**Where.** `public/app.js:3934`: every load input is `value = '0'`. `fleeRefusal` accepts an empty load; `flee` zeroes every

**Direction.** Pre-fill the inputs with `packFlight`'s load.

### 4.4 S15 — The spring's news is told to everybody at once *(re-read)*

each family hears; let a rumour be wrong sometimes.

### 4.7 S18 — Staying is treated more gently than going
