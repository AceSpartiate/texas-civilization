# San Antonio de Béxar illustrated assembly

The local art assembly in `public/bexar-layout.js` uses the existing illustrated asset library. It follows the supplied George Nelson panorama's broad relationships: the twin plazas and San Fernando in the western town, the winding river, the Alamo on the opposite bank, the Plaza de Valero outside its south entrance, and the tree-lined Alameda extending east. The second supplied image guides the Alamo compound's complete enclosing ranges and large open court; its perspective is not used as a survey.

The [GLO catalogue entry for map 83600](https://historictexasmaps.com/collection/search-results/83600-san-antonio-de-bexar-1836-general-map-collection) identifies the supplied panorama. This reconstruction does **not** establish surveyed street courses, lot boundaries, individual occupants, exact building count, or exact distances between monuments. It depicts no dated army deployment or casualty event. The named civic buildings use generic existing silhouettes; San Fernando's `chapel` sprite is a visual stand-in, not a claim to reproduce its 1836 façade.

## Integration contract

Implemented 2026-09-14: the real-map game's **Béxar** camera button opens the existing projected town. At close range its generic settlement cluster is replaced by this assembly. Ground anchors convert local feet to world miles around the town at local (1200,2050). Buildings join the normal depth-sorted draw list; no decorative people or simulated households are added. The normal game retains its real-map river geometry rather than substituting this illustrative river course. Regional views continue to use the settlement marker. Random prairie scatter is suppressed within the detailed assembly so trees do not grow through civic buildings. The riverbank trees are left out in the game (`bankTrees:false`), since they line the illustrative river the game does not draw and would otherwise ring bare grass; the Alameda trees stay. Unlabelled houses carry no `label` key, so the generated manifest and the layout agree.

**The Alamo on the map, 2026-09-18.** The live map no longer draws the compound as flat boxes and 10-ft wall pieces beside town houses drawn 57-105 ft tall: `bexarDrawables` draws it from `alamoMassing` (`public/alamo-layout.js`) on its true footprint, at its true heights times the towns' `DRAWN_HEIGHT`, with flat parapeted roofs, the library's painted stone on the faces the camera sees, outlines and shadows as the sprites have, and the plaza solid like the town's plazas. The compound's dimensions were corrected to the record at the same time (`HIST-TEX-090` to `-092`, [ALAMO_LAYOUT.md](ALAMO_LAYOUT.md) "On the map"); its placement and transform are unchanged. The workshop keeps its own oblique renderer.

Open `/alamo-workshop.html` and choose **San Antonio de Béxar** for the full reference composition, including its reconstructed river and Alameda. This view uses the full existing Alamo renderer and the same layout object; room focus returns to cutaway. The supplied compound image also informed a correction to contiguous west-range rooms, replacing the former seven-foot gaps without changing perimeter dimensions or room IDs. The preview contains no dated troop formations, burning or casualties from the reference's battle setting.

The generated art manifest now contains `assemblies.bexar`, with all 80 building placements, 144 trees, three plazas, nine roads, river and shared Alamo transform. `node scripts/bexar-browser-proof.mjs` passes real-map host rendering, the town camera button, asset loading and phone width. The new button initially exposed horizontal overflow on phones; allowing the navigation buttons to wrap fixed it. The expanded Alamo proof passes town overview, room movement, destruction and reduced motion. Full suite: 379 tests passed before the final registry metadata refresh; registry checks rerun afterward.

`BEXAR_LAYOUT` exports feet in a north-up local coordinate plane, positive y south, with bounds 4,000 × 3,400 feet. `buildings` and `props` contain stable IDs, existing sprite IDs, x/y ground anchors and `heightFeet` for the illustrator's apparent sprite height. The latter is not a measured roof elevation or building collision footprint. Render buildings with the normal art-library ground anchor and sort by y. `plazas` are rectangles; `river` and `roads` provide point polylines and widths in feet. `crossings` identifies the one illustrated road crossing; it does not authorize a world-level traversal shortcut.

`alamo` contains the **same imported `ALAMO_LAYOUT` object**, positioned at (2650,550) with rotation 0 degrees. `alamoToBexar` and `bexarToAlamo` apply the reversible translation/rotation. The compound retains its original dimensions, room identities, roof cutaways and north-wall damage state. Do not flatten it into a replacement sprite or create a second saved Alamo world. Town-scale artwork and compound close-up must agree on this transform.

Vegetation and building placement are deterministic. Fixed riverbank trees avoid road corridors and buildings. River-course and building placements are intentionally separated. Existing premade adobe and tiled buildings establish the town's shared game style; two jacal assets vary La Villita. No raster asset was generated or altered for this assembly.

## Limits for continuation

Verification on 2026-09-14: `node --test tests/bexar-layout.test.mjs` passes. The bundled test checks real atlas references, unique placement IDs, identical imported compound geometry, preserved wall lengths, reversible coordinates and building clearance from water. Six isolated source-copy regressions were each caught: missing sprite, duplicate placement ID, a copied rather than shared compound, doubled x scale, a ten-foot inverse offset and a church moved into the river. The injected copies were loaded through data URLs; no working production module or shared test data was mutated, and the temporary test file was removed afterward.

This file supplies visual geography, not historical collision data or population. World integration must project only locally known people/events. Do not populate it with invented global news, duplicate existing person IDs, or infer hidden enemy positions from decorative figures. A contextual art preview may show named landmarks; normal player knowledge remains server-controlled. If later gameplay requires exact street fighting, research and version the geometry separately before treating these illustrative plots as authoritative navigation.
