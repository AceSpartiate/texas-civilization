# Gonzales historical foundation

Scope: the confrontation near Gonzales from September 29 through October 2, 1835, plus the cannon's essential 1831 background, the geography of the Gonzales country, and a generalized province layer for the rest of Texas. Research checked September 9, 2026. Maturity: **PROTOTYPE**. This is the bounded research basis for the thin Gonzales slice, not a history of the entire Revolution.

Read `VISION.md` first. Historical outcomes constrain the world; they do not require a household to participate. The director must preserve the outcome whether every player accepts, refuses, arrives late, or stays home. The help request, its personal consequences, and the family perspective are fictional teaching devices.

## Claim registry

Keep these IDs stable. Attach the relevant IDs to historical world events and battle outcomes so a later developer can trace a claim without relying on conversation history. An implementation note defines the permitted use of a claim; it does not assert that an unfinished feature exists.

Here **DOCUMENTED** means the specific factual anchor is established in the cited institutional history. **STRONGLY SUPPORTED** identifies contextual synthesis. The research pass read the institutional accounts below, not original handwritten military reports; do not describe this as a completed primary-source transcription project. **FICTIONAL FOR GAMEPLAY** marks deliberate inventions, and **DISPUTED** marks a detail that must not be asserted as settled fact in this slice.

### Province details deliberately excluded

- **Any coordinate as a real position.** The province layout is arranged to match documented relationships and nothing more. It is not a projection, and no two places on it are a measured distance apart.
- **"The only natural lake in Texas."** Caddo Lake is documented as beginning as a natural lake and later dammed ([TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/caddo-lake/history)), but the widely repeated "only natural lake" claim was **NOT FOUND** on any institutional page checked. Do not assert it. **Draw no other lakes:** the large Texas lakes are twentieth-century reservoirs and did not exist in 1835.
- **County names before 1836.** Several places are described by modern sources in terms of counties the Republic did not create until March 1836 or later; during the Mexican period these were municipalities. Do not label 1835 administration with county names.
- **"Flows southeast" for the Rio Grande and the Brazos.** Their entries give rise and mouth but never state direction in those words. The trend is an inference and must not be quoted as sourced fact.
- **A single elevation figure for the Edwards Plateau**, and **a terrain adjective for Gonzales County as a whole**, were both **NOT FOUND**. Do not supply either.
