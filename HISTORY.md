# Gonzales historical foundation

Scope: the confrontation near Gonzales from September 29 through October 2, 1835, plus the cannon's essential 1831 background, the geography of the Gonzales country, and a generalized province layer for the rest of Texas. Research checked September 9, 2026. Maturity: **PROTOTYPE**. This is the bounded research basis for the thin Gonzales slice, not a history of the entire Revolution.

Read `VISION.md` first. Historical outcomes constrain the world; they do not require a household to participate. The director must preserve the outcome whether every player accepts, refuses, arrives late, or stays home. The help request, its personal consequences, and the family perspective are fictional teaching devices.

### Province details deliberately excluded

- **Any coordinate as a real position.** The province layout is arranged to match documented relationships and nothing more. It is not a projection, and no two places on it are a measured distance apart.
- **"The only natural lake in Texas."** Caddo Lake is documented as beginning as a natural lake and later dammed ([TPWD](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/caddo-lake/history)), but the widely repeated "only natural lake" claim was **NOT FOUND** on any institutional page checked. Do not assert it. **Draw no other lakes:** the large Texas lakes are twentieth-century reservoirs and did not exist in 1835.
- **County names before 1836.** Several places are described by modern sources in terms of counties the Republic did not create until March 1836 or later; during the Mexican period these were municipalities. Do not label 1835 administration with county names.
- **"Flows southeast" for the Rio Grande and the Brazos.** Their entries give rise and mouth but never state direction in those words. The trend is an inference and must not be quoted as sourced fact.
- **A single elevation figure for the Edwards Plateau**, and **a terrain adjective for Gonzales County as a whole**, were both **NOT FOUND**. Do not supply either.

### Details deliberately excluded

**Artillery identity and exact depiction — DISPUTED / not resolved for this foundation.** The updated TSHA cannon entry distinguishes a bronze cannon involved in the return dispute from a smaller iron piece and describes evidence for two guns. The museum account centers the small displayed cannon. This slice therefore makes no precise claim about caliber, dimensions, surviving artifact identity, number of guns fired, or exact shot sequence. A representative cannon communicates the dispute without claiming a forensic reconstruction. [TSHA artillery discussion](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/gonzales-come-and-take-it-cannon); [Gonzales Memorial Museum account](https://www.gonzales.texas.gov/p/departments/gonzales-memorial-museum).

**Geography deliberately not asserted.** The 2026-09-09 research pass looked for each of the following and did not find it in an institutional source. None may be stated as fact, and none may be quietly introduced by drawing it on a map:

- **Distance from Gonzales to Béxar.** NOT FOUND. One non-institutional site gives 169 miles for Byrd Lockhart's whole surveyed road, ambiguous as to whether that covers the Gonzales–Béxar segment alone or the full San Felipe–Gonzales–Béxar route. Do not print a mileage. Relative travel times may be modelled as gameplay abstraction under `FIC-GONZ-003`.
- **A name for the ford.** NOT FOUND. "The ford" is documented; a proper name is not. Do not invent one.
- **The "seven squares" town plan** and any 49-block street grid. Supported only by a non-institutional source and not confirmed on any TSHA, GLO or THC page. Draw a modest cluster of buildings; do not draw a specific surveyed street grid.
- **Population of Gonzales or the colony in 1835.** NOT FOUND. The nearest documented figures are 531 colonists colony-wide in early 1831 and 32 structures in the town by 1836. Do not state a 1835 population.
- **Deer, bear or turkey as local game.** NOT FOUND for this locality. Only buffalo is documented near Gonzales (1828). Any other hunted animal is `FICTIONAL FOR GAMEPLAY` and must be labelled as such, not presented as documented fauna.
- **Distance of the original 1825 townsite from the present city.** DISPUTED. TSHA's own entry gives both "about a mile east" and "three miles from the present Gonzales", and the city gives 2½ miles east of the river confluence. Do not resolve this by picking one.
- **The number of DeWitt titles issued.** DISPUTED between 180 and 189. Not needed for gameplay; do not cite either.

Omission of modeled casualties does not assert that nobody was harmed historically.

## Implementation and review contract

1. Historical timeline/world truth uses the stable `HIST-GONZ-*` IDs. Private reports reference that truth only when delivered; public Host knowledge is separately controlled.
2. The pressure request references the historical situation as a cause but carries a fiction classification. Acceptance and refusal both produce valid story state.
3. A participating principal leaves the same home as the same entity, travels, arrives, and receives an understandable personal consequence. The historical battle does not wait for or depend on that individual.
4. The battle resolves automatically to `HIST-GONZ-004`, with the calendar anchor in `HIST-GONZ-003`. Player actions cannot flip the historical winner or recover the cannon for the Mexican detachment.
5. Preserve the personal consequence and its causal event chain across save/reload, reconnect, and any later time compression. Do not quietly return absent people, borrowed property, or unresolved service commitments home.
6. Before expanding narration or art, compare its factual claims with this registry. Add checked sources for genuinely new claims; do not convert a convenient placeholder into asserted history by polishing its presentation.

Recommended review evidence: historical date/outcome tests independent of participation; refusal-path test; private/public report isolation; same-ID travel; persistent consequence after restart; and Event Log trace from historical situation through knowledge, request, choice, action, consequence, and memory. Refer to `HANDOFF.md` for the actual executed gate evidence rather than treating this research contract as a test result.
