                  STEP 1 OF 4 · THE BEGINNING

                  Roll for your family

                  The die decides how many are in your family and who they are. It is thrown once. There is no second roll, so the family it gives you is the family you play.

                  One throw. A whole life ahead.
                  BEFORE THE CLASS BEGINS

                  Pack the wagon

                  What you load is what the family arrives with. Tools, seed and powder can be bought in a town later, if there is coin; the things for the house cannot be bought anywhere.

                  You can change all of this until your teacher presses Start. After that the wagon is packed.

                              STEP 2 OF 4

                              Name your family

                              Your family was already here before you joined, so there is no die to roll. What it has done so far stays in its journal under the names it had then; from now on it goes by the names you give it.

                              Everybody in the family carries it. It is chosen once and cannot be changed afterwards; first names can be changed at any time, on the family panel.

                              How we look

                              Your family, your look.

                              Children are not asked for: they inherit your family's colors and grow into their own age-appropriate figures. Chosen once, a look stays with them. Appearance changes nothing in the game.
