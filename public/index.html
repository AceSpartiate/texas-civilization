Family: Texas 1835/36

A family of settlers in the last year of Mexican Texas.

Your family

You are one family among the settlers of Texas in the autumn of 1835. First you make them, in four steps: roll the die for who they are, give the family a last name, name each of them, and choose how the parents look.

From the moment the wagon starts in, the game walks you through the farm one task at a time - the house, the field, the crop, the town, the hunt and the well. The country is yours to work once that is done, or at once if you press the X on the guide to stop it.

Name each of them

Step 3 of 4. They are already named: change any you like, or press Continue and keep them. You can rename anybody later on the family panel.
